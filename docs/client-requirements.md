## 2. Business Overview

Cedar & Stone Home Services is a locally operated residential maintenance and improvement company serving homeowners and landlords in the Bangor area.

The company focuses on practical home repairs, routine maintenance, smaller improvement projects, and seasonal property needs.

Cedar & Stone Home Services is intended for customers who need dependable help with common residential projects that may be too complex, time-consuming, or inconvenient to complete on their own.

The company does not position itself as a large commercial construction company.

Its focus is on smaller residential projects, careful workmanship, clear communication, and dependable service.

# 18. Project Example 1 — Entry Stair Repair

## Project Title

Entry Stair and Handrail Repair

### Problem

A homeowner had loose stair trim and a damaged handrail near the main entry.

The loose components created both a visual problem and a potential safety concern.

### Work Performed

The damaged wood was removed where necessary.

Replacement trim was fitted, the handrail was secured, and the repaired areas were prepared for finishing.

### Result

The entry stairs became more stable, safer to use, and visually consistent with the surrounding area.

---

# 19. Project Example 2 — Backyard Deck Refresh

## Project Title

Backyard Deck Maintenance and Board Replacement

### Problem

Several deck boards were worn, beginning to split, and showing signs of seasonal exposure.

Some fasteners had also become loose.

### Work Performed

Damaged boards were removed and replaced where appropriate.

Loose fasteners were corrected and the deck was cleaned and prepared for continued seasonal maintenance.

### Result

The deck became safer, more functional, and better prepared for continued outdoor use.

---

# 20. Project Example 3 — Rental Property Fixture Update

## Project Title

Rental Property Fixture and Hardware Update

### Problem

A landlord needed several worn household fixtures and hardware items replaced before a new tenant moved into the property.

### Work Performed

Approved fixtures and hardware were removed and replaced.

The surrounding work areas were inspected and cleaned after installation.

### Result

The rental unit was updated and prepared for tenant occupancy.

---

# 21. Project Example 4 — Interior Room Refresh

## Project Title

Living Room Paint Refresh

### Problem

The walls of a living room had visible wear, marks, and an outdated color.

### Work Performed
